QATT Brush 2

A library of stroke forms: each has a bend, a kind of start and end (flat, pointed or kihitsu) and a gradient for its width. Every symbol is made of three of them between two end points. Width, angle and size of the ends are the same for all forms. Your work is kept in this browser; export it as JSON to keep a copy. The first time, the symbols of the first brush editor are taken over.

A dot after the name marks a symbol you changed. Until you change it, a centred form follows its rhyme form.

Violet frame: the em box of the font cell, 1000 × 1000 (Noto Sans CJK), its lower edge is the ideographic baseline. Dark red frame: the ICF box (ideographic character face), where the glyph should sit. Dashed line: the Latin baseline. Green dotted frame: the 0–100 window in which the letters of qatt.js were drawn; the placement below (offset and scale) puts it into the ICF box. Blue frame: the extent of dense glyphs in Noto Sans CJK (margin about 3.5 % at the sides, bottom edge 80 units below the baseline); dashed blue: the outermost overshoot of any Noto glyph.

Edit
Drag

Choose a stroke (A, B, C) or a mark and what a drag moves, then drag anywhere on the canvas; everything else stays locked. The filled point is where the brush starts. Shift while moving a point keeps the length, Alt stops snapping; the arrow keys move in small steps.

Strokes

Drag the square handle to bend the stroke. The round ones only change this preview (direction and length).

Context

The parts drawn around the symbol in the editor and in the combinations.

Brush

Save

Changes are saved in this browser automatically. The export also holds the finished outlines of all paths.
