PA editor

Type Quốc Ngữ in Telex, each word becomes PA when you press the space bar. The PA script

A draft. Words are split by the parser of the site and written by the rules of the specification. The font is loaded from the qtng/cdn repository. Backspace and the cursor treat a PA cell (one square) as one character.

Reading

Telex: aa â, aw ă, ee ê, oo ô, ow ơ, uw ư, dd đ; s sắc, f huyền, r hỏi, x ngã, j nặng.
