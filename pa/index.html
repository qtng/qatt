Guide

What is PA?

Phonetic Annotation (PA, in Vietnamese Phiên Âm tự) writes how Vietnamese is pronounced. Its signs are built like Hán Nôm characters: 23 basic characters stand for the onsets, and 19 vowel and tone marks extend them. Every Vietnamese syllable can be written, whatever the Chữ Nôm or Quốc Ngữ spelling is.

The glyphs on this page are set in the earlier PA font. Download the font or type text in the online editor.

How a syllable is written

A syllable is split into four parts: the onset (the consonant at the start), the vowel (with its glide), the coda (the consonant or semivowel at the end) and the tone. For example 船 thuyền (boat) is th + uyê + n + the low (huyền) tone.

The parts are written in at most two blocks:

  • The first block joins the onset (a Hán Nôm character on the left) and the vowel (a mark on the right). Every syllable has it.
  • The second block holds the coda and the tone: the coda is a Hán Nôm character, the tone a small mark in it. A syllable with a tone but no coda gets the tone mark alone; a syllable with neither has no second block.

Optionally the two blocks can be stacked into one square, like a Chữ Nôm character (see the joiner in the specification). The next font will offer both forms.

Specification

Draft. Version 0.1. The specification defines the character repertoire, its code points and how a font composes the glyphs. The code points are provisional and lie in the Supplementary Private Use Area-A. There is no reference font yet, so the tables of the specification show no glyphs.

Syllable grammar

A syllable is written with the four kinds of component of a Vietnamese syllable: onset (A), nucleus (N), coda (C) and tone (T), and an optional joiner (J). The nucleus includes the glide: oa, uy and similar are nuclei of their own.

Syllable  = Stem [ [ J ] Extension ]
Stem      = A N
Extension = C [ T ]  |  T
  • A stem always has an onset. A syllable without an onset uses the onset _, which has a shape of its own.
  • A coda without a tone is a valid extension (tai, ngang tone). A tone alone is a valid extension (an open syllable with a tone).
  • A nucleus never stands without an onset. An onset may stand alone (a lone consonant).

The joiner J chooses the layout, and it is optional:

  • Without J a syllable has at most two cells side by side: the stem cell (A and N in one glyph) and the extension cell (C and T in one glyph, or C alone, or T alone).
  • With J between stem and extension both form one cell: the stem above, the extension below, in one em square like a CJK character. This is the form for vertical text and for mixing with Chữ Nôm.

J is only valid between a stem and an extension.

Composition

A font renders the sequences with glyph substitution (OpenType ccmp). The classes tell what belongs together within a cell, so no control character is needed there. The joiner is the only one: it joins two cells into one.

SequenceResult
A Nstem glyph (506)
C Textension glyph with coda and tone (60)
Aonset cell alone (23)
Ccoda cell alone (10)
Ttone cell alone (6)
A N J E (E = C T, C or T)one cell: the stem glyph squeezed into the upper part, the extension glyph squeezed into the lower part
N without A in frontnot valid; an input method inserts the onset _

A stem followed by a tone alone (A N T) is two cells. Every cell is one em wide.

The joined cell

The joined cell is not a precomposed glyph for every pair, which would be 506 × 76 = 38,456 glyphs. Like the stack of QATT the font swaps both parts for squeezed forms: the stem becomes its upper form (506 glyphs), the joiner a glyph without width, and the extension its lower form (76 glyphs: 60 coda and tone, 10 codas, 6 tones) with no advance, drawn over the cell of the stem. The parts are squeezed in height only, the strokes keep their width.

One font with QATT

PA is designed to share one font file with QATT. For that the two scripts must not touch each other:

  • Code points. QATT uses U+F0000..U+F00AF, PA U+F0800..U+F085F. Nothing overlaps, and QATT keeps room to grow.
  • Control characters. PA has its own joiner (U+F0860) and no combiner. The QATT joiner (U+F009A), combiner (U+F009B) and tone marks (U+F009C..U+F00A3) have no meaning in PA text, and a PA tone is not a QATT tone mark.
  • Rules. The substitutions of each script act only on the classes of that script (@A @N @C @T for PA), so a QATT cell next to a PA cell is never combined with it. The two sets of rules can sit in the same ccmp feature as separate lookups.
  • Size. PA adds 1,188 glyphs: 605 for the cells without the joiner (506 stems, 60 extensions with coda and tone, 39 single cells), 582 squeezed forms for the joined cell (506 upper, 76 lower) and the joiner. That is well inside the glyph limit of a font next to the QATT forms and pairs.
  • Metrics. Both scripts use the same square cell, so mixed text keeps one line height and advance.

Reading Quốc Ngữ

A syllable of Quốc Ngữ is split into onset, nucleus, coda and tone, then mapped to the names above.

  • Tone. The accent of the vowel gives the tone and is removed: sắc 1, huyền 2, hỏi 3, ngã 4, nặng 5. No accent: no tone.
  • Onset. k and q are c; gh is g; ngh is ng; d is dz; đ is d; g before i is gi. No onset is _.
  • Glide. qu is the onset c plus a glide nucleus (quô is read as uâ). The glide forms oa, oă, uơ, uâ, uê, oe, uy, uyê are the nuclei wa, wae, war, waer, we, wer, wi, wir.
  • Coda. o after the nucleus is U, y is I.
  • Short a. a before the codas u, y, nh, ch is written with the nucleus ae (ă).

Open points

  1. Tone after a stem. The drawings have two forms: a small tone inside the stem cell and a full-size tone as a cell of its own. Proposal: the full-size cell is the default (as in the earlier font); the small form is chosen with the variation selector U+FE00 after the tone.
  2. Joined cell. The proportions of the stack (proposal: stem 55 %, extension 45 % of the height, as in the QATT stack) and whether the parts keep their width or are narrowed. The character: an own joiner U+F0860 (as QATT has) or the standard zero width joiner U+200D, which a fallback font shows as nothing.
  3. Tone 6. The drawings have a sixth tone mark; its meaning is not yet decided (for example an explicit level tone).
  4. Combinations that Vietnamese does not use (for instance b with a glide nucleus). Proposal: the font draws all 506 stems, the input method decides what is offered.
