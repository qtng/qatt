Type QATT

Type Vietnamese (Quốc Ngữ). When a word is finished (space, punctuation or Enter) it is replaced by the characters of the QATT Unicode block, which the font shows as Quốc Âm Tân Tự. The result is plain text: copy it anywhere where the QATT font is available. Words that have no letter in the chosen reading of the rhyme table stay as typed. See also QATT Editor, which draws the words with qatt.js.
