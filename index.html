What is Quốc Âm Tân Tự?

Quốc Âm Tân Tự means “new script for the sounds of the national language”. It was created in the 1840s, under Emperor Thiệu Trị. Only one text survives, in two manuscripts in Hanoi.

The script has 22 basic shapes of three strokes each. A small extra stroke in one of six places gives six more forms. Two shapes side by side make one syllable: the left one is its beginning, the right one its ending. Small circles and half circles in the corners mark the eight tones.

Why Unicode?

A script that cannot yet be written as text.

Unicode has no characters for Quốc Âm Tân Tự yet. Today it can only be shown as images or with the private font of this site. Once encoded, it can be typed, searched, quoted and shared on every device, next to Hán Nôm and Quốc ngữ.

Where we stand

The road to Unicode

  1. Sources: the two manuscripts in Hanoi (AB.630), transcribed page by page. In progress.
  2. Repertoire: 164 characters and the encoding model, draft Alpha 1. Draft done.
  3. Font and tools: a font, an editor and a converter, for now in the Private Use Area. Done.
  4. Open questions: scholarship and some character properties. In progress.
  5. Submission: the proposal to the Unicode Technical Committee. Not yet.
