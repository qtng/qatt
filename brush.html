QATT Brush

Lay brush strokes over the centre lines of the QATT shapes and see every combination change while you edit. Your work is kept in this browser; export it as JSON to keep a copy.

A dot after the name marks a path you changed.

Drag a stroke to move it, the round handles to move its ends and the square ones to bend it. Arrow keys move the selected stroke or point (Shift 2, Alt ¼); hold Alt while dragging to stop snapping.

Context

The parts drawn around the selected path in the editor and in the combinations.

Brush

Stroke forms

A stroke form applies to every stroke that uses it. Widths are in units of the brush weight.

Save

Changes are saved in this browser automatically. The export also holds the finished outlines of all paths.
