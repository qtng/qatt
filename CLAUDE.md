# qtng/qatt

The QATT site (Quốc Âm Tân Tự, a script for Vietnamese): `index.html`, `symbols.html`, `editor.html`, `spell.html` ("Spell it!", vi "Đánh vần!", the name of the game: spell a word: onset and rhyme shapes together on 6 buttons in two rows of 3 (the 22 base shapes are in rows of 8, 8 and 6 in the order of `symbols.html` (`ROWS`); the rows of the onset and of the rhyme are the two rows of the word, one row alone gets the next one (the one above for the last); each is filled up to 3 shapes at random, in the order of the row), then the tone; the words are the one-syllable ranked entries of `chunom/dict.csv`, loaded from the Chunom site (word levels, `HARD_RHYMES` in the page: the skill badge of the player, with 2.5 = upper intermediate between intermediate and advanced (intermediate and the usual rhyme time of the decoding under the limit of the badge, the rhyme being the hardest of the three kinds); words with a stop final (p, c, t, ch, `STOP_FINALS`) and with the rhymes ông, ong, ung, uông only from intermediate (all decodings; 20% of the words are drawn from them at that level, `FOCUS_SHARE`), those with ôm, om, um, uôm only with the decoding qatt.org and from intermediate too, those with ao, oao, eo, oeo, yu, uyu, êu, iêu only with qatt.org and from upper intermediate (at each level 20% of the words are drawn from the rhymes unlocked there); the rhyme is that of the spelling and a stop counts as its nasal twin, ốc = ông; from intermediate the shape buttons are shown in random order, from upper intermediate also the marked forms (`shuffled`, only the order, not the choice); at the end of the page `#levels` lists the added difficulties from the easiest and whether each is active, `showLevels`); score: +10 a right choice, −2 a wrong one, a bonus for a solved word that falls from 50 to 0 in the minute, a cheer and +25 for a quick word; the speed of the player per kind of symbol (onset, rhyme, tone; an onset or rhyme cell is one item, its time the mean of the clicks of its shape and its marked form; a wrong click adds `CAP_ADD` 2 s to the time of its step (the time from the start of the step to the right click, the wrong clicks in it), whose right click shows the kind; it is no time of its own (a penalty of the own time + 2 s fed the own time and grew with it; the times learned anew once, `relearn1`: n down to `MIN_SAMPLES`)) as exponentially smoothed times (slow `ALPHA` 0.01 (about the last 100 times) for the server and the ranking, faster `ALPHA_FAST` 0.2 (after 10 equal times about 90% of the way) for the display, the card and +50 at a milestone such as onsets under 2 s; a time counts at most as the own slow value + 2 s (at most 10 s; 10 s until `MIN_SAMPLES`); the slow and the fast value stop at 10 s; the hint of the right button comes after the own value of that kind + 5 s without a click (5 s until `MIN_SAMPLES`), a step with a hint does not count in the speed; from `MIN_SAMPLES` = 5 times of a kind they count: quick words, milestones, later the ranking); Skip: the word does not count at all (score and times as before the word), then Skip is locked until a word is solved whose points beat the smoothed points of the words so far (`avg`); for a signed-in player (not an anonymous one; `SupabaseService.initQuiet`, no anonymous account is made) the points and the speed go to the server (`tools/db/spell.sql`: `spell_scores`, `spell_speed`, `spell_submit`, `spell_leaderboard`, project ChunomApp; the integrity of the requests is described in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private`), and the ranking under the game reads from there (shown only to admins for now, `RANKING_PUBLIC` in the page); every 4 solved words (`STATS_EVERY`) a card shows the form of those last words only (no long-term value): the gauge (needle, "Form", 0 to 100) compares the round with the player's own usual time of every kind, the slow value v when the card before was shown (`me.base`), against the geometric mean of the times of the round (measured, without the deductions; a wrong click adds 2 s to its step, a step with a hint is a slow time; the geometric mean damps a single slow click): 50 is a round as usual, 100 one `FORM_SPAN` (1.5) times as fast, 0 one 1.5 times as slow, with few times nearer to 50 (n / (n + `FORM_DAMP` 2) of the difference), the mean of the kinds; a kind without its usual time yet (fewer than `MIN_SAMPLES`) takes the scale of the stars; so an ordinary round is always in the middle, whatever the level, and there is no ceiling; below it, per kind of symbol, stars for the level (absolute): the same geometric mean as the status line shows it, a star for each time of `STAR_STEPS` of its kind it beats (0 to 5; every kind has its own times, even steps around an ordinary round: onset 0.5 s, rhyme 1.55 s, tone 2.9 s shown; the milestones of the points, `MILESTONES`, stay as they are), the mean time, an arrow ↑ ↓ against the card before and ✗n for the wrong choices; the card rewards: the stars light up one after the other (the new ones against the card before glow), the number runs up with the needle, sparks by the result (green many, yellow a few, red none; not with reduced motion), a badge "New best form!" (`me.best`) and the gain since the last card (`me.lastValue`), and a line with the kind of the best form of the round, without stars ("Best form: …"; below 50 an encouragement); the game starts with a click on Start (`#startGame`; no word, speech or clock before it; `?word=` too); `?decoding=qatt|omniglot` preselects that reading of the rhyme table (an unknown name is ignored) and follows the dropdown (`history.replaceState`, no new history entry); a new word is read aloud when it appears (sound on); closing the card starts a new word when one was in play under it (that word is dropped); the timer that swaps the cells for the solution one second after a right tone (`winTimer`) is cancelled by `start()` (a quick click on Next must not show the old solution over the new word); above the score (no star icon any more) a small copy of the skill badge of `spell-stats.html` (`showBadge`: the server's `spell_scores.badge` from `spellLoad`, raised at once by the usual times of the browser by the same rule, shown 0 to 3; `me.badge` is kept in `localStorage.qattSpell`); the whole card takes the colours of the result (the Bootstrap classes `alert-danger` / `-warning` / `-success` for the text, the subtle background and the border, the limits 40 and 70 of the arcs of the gauge) while the needle swings (`me.round`, `me.prev`; a skipped word takes its times back); at the bottom of the page, for admins only (`isAdmin` of `js/fonts.js`), a log (stopwatch): the first line is the word in play with the time of every click (✗ for a wrong one) and the running one, the lines below the earlier words, to check the measuring; the status line also shows the last time of every kind after an arrow; what is shown (status line, card, gauge, milestones) takes `CLICK_TIME` off every kind (the time to find and press a button: 0.5 s, for a tone 1 s because its 8 marks, 4 circles and 4 half circles, differ only by their place; a rhyme is the mean of its clicks, so it is the same off the mean) and `READ_TIME` 1.25 s more off the onset (1.75 s in all) (the time to read the word is in its first click; at least `MIN_SHOWN` 0.1 s), what is kept, sent to the server and shown in the stopwatch is the measured time; a visitor sees the points rise but the score stays 0 (they are kept in `shadow`), is asked to sign in at 60, 300 and 1000 points with a "Not now" button, and nothing is taken over at the first sign-in; the browser keeps (`localStorage.qattSpell`: `{ score, decoding, stats: { <decoding>: { onset, rhyme, tone } } }`, the times kept per decoding) so far, constants `POINTS`, `CAP`, `ALPHA` in the page), `spell-stats.html` (noindex, "My statistics": points, words and the skill badge between them, the speed per decoding, the chart and the table of the cards of progress from `spell_history`; the badge is `spell_scores.badge` of `tools/db/spell.sql`: how many of the three kinds have a usual time under 1.75 s as the game shows it (raw onset < 3.5 s, rhyme < 2.25 s, tone < 2.75 s, from 5 times), 0 newbie, 1 beginner, 2 intermediate, 3 advanced, only ever raised by `spell_write_speed`; the database allows 0 to 5, the page shows 0 to 3 for now), `decodings.html`, `unicode.html`, `wiki.html`, `vandao/`, `admin/`, with `js/`, `vendor/` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase, shared by both sites), `fonts/`, `img/`.
Served at the **root** of `qatt.org`. `js/navigation.js` is the menu (and the translation `Lang`) of both sites; the pages of the Chunom site load it from here. `404.html` forwards old `/qatt/…` addresses to the same path without the prefix and old `/chunom/`, `/chunom-assets/`, `/pages/` addresses to the Chunom site (prefix kept); the countdown is 2 seconds; the old `/practice/` and `/app.html` (the former practice of the script, removed) go to `spell.html`.
`css/site.css` is what all pages share whatever the theme (colour variables, the padding of `btn-pill`); load it right after Bootstrap (`<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/site.css">`, the path as for `vendor/`). The look of buttons (`btn-pill`, `btn-cinnabar`, `btn-accent`, `btn-quiet`, `btn-ink`), dropdowns and the other shared elements is in a **theme file**, see below. Bootstrap draws buttons from `--bs-btn-*` variables, so set those (not `border-color`) to change the colours of a button. The pages of the Chunom site under `/chunom/` link it too (`https://qatt.org/css/site.css`).
Buttons never wrap (`.btn { white-space: nowrap }` in `css/site.css`), also in Vietnamese.
For admins (`app_metadata.is_admin`, as for the palette button) the menu bar also shows a shield icon (`.admin-link`) that links to `https://qatt.org/admin/`.
`js/navigation.js` also adds a round floating button (`.to-top`, in the colour of the bar `--nav-bg`, an arrow up) that scrolls to the top after 400px of scrolling; on the phone it sits above the bottom bar.

Day and night (on the Chunom site only; qatt.org has no switch and shows `nocturne` unless a theme was chosen): by the clock of the visitor it is night from 22:00 to 06:00, in the time zones of Vietnam (`Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`, `Asia/Saigon`) from 18:00 to 06:00 (`NIGHT_DEFAULT`, `NIGHT_RULES` in `Theme`; start must be later than end) and the theme `nocturne` is shown instead of the chosen one; the dark themes (`nocturne`, `future`; `DARK_THEMES` in `Theme`) also set `data-bs-theme="dark"` on `<html>`, so Bootstrap's own components get dark colours too; the icon left of EN | VI in the menu bar (sun by day, moon by night; the moon is yellow like the admin icons) shows the time of day and everybody can click it: it then switches by hand (the palette button for choosing a theme stays for admins), and that choice holds only until the next change between day and night, then the clock decides again (`localStorage.daynight1`: `{ mode, key }`, key = the date the period began plus `d` or `n`, so a night after midnight still belongs to the evening before; void when the key is no longer the current one); `?theme=<name>` shows that theme whatever the time; `Theme.dayNight` and the event `daynightchange`. The editor of `editor.html` and of `writer.html` of the Chunom site is one: `js/qatt-writer.js` (`QattWriter.mount(element, { switch, storage })`, loaded by both pages from qatt.org; the QATT toolbar above the card (decoding, font, Convert QATT, Variants = three small buttons (`#variants`, each a middot and off) for the characters before the cursor: each has a place of its own (left Marked 1, middle Neutral tone, right Tham Thư) and shows the characters as they will stand (a preview, in the QATT font) when it applies, a click does it: Tham Thư (stacks the two characters before the cursor by putting a combiner U+F009B between them; only when the cursor is behind two letters U+F0000..U+F0099 whose second is a single one (tone marks behind it may stand) and is not the form with stroke 1 and the first is not the upper part of a stack), Marked 1 (the letter before the cursor is an unmarked form that stands alone (not the second part of a pair or of a stack: the form with stroke 1 is never part of one): its code point + 1, the form with marking stroke 1, which has no reading of its own: Convert writes a single such letter (with or without tone marks) as the reading of the unmarked form in capitals, TÔNG, and reads a word of two letters or more in capitals whose lowercase is one unmarked letter back as that letter + 1, also while typing), Neutral tone (the cursor is right behind a letter: the mark of ngang U+F009C is put behind it); Convert converts only cells that have a reading (a cell without one, e.g. a pair with a letter of stroke 1, stays as it is), the mark of the neutral tone stays behind the word as a character of its own and becomes part of the cell again on the way back; centred, only while QATT is on, its space stays so the card does not move; one row, never wraps), the size in the card, the field, Telex, vertical text, Example and the cells kept whole; Convert reads Tham Thư apart, as if the combiner were not there: the parts of a stack are read one by one and the combiner becomes the IDS character ⿱ (U+2FF1) between the readings, and ⿱ between QATT cells becomes the combiner again; the variant buttons keep the focus in the field; needs `parser.js`, `qatt-text.js`, `fonts.js` and `ime.js` of the Chunom site). The pages keep only header, card colours and the text around. The difference is the switch: `switch: 'nom'` (qatt.org: QATT always on, "Chữ Nôm" turns the candidate list on, off at the start) and `switch: 'qatt'` (Chunom: candidates always on, "Quốc Âm Tân Tự" turns QATT on, off at the start). A change to the editor is made there, once, and needs a look at both pages (and in the themes). Both pages load it with `?v=N` (`editor.html` here, `writer.html` in `qtng/chunom`): raise N in both with every change of `js/qatt-writer.js`, the file is cached for 4 hours (GitHub Pages `max-age=14400`), and a visitor of chunom.org cannot refresh a file of another domain.
The fonts are cached for 4 hours too: `FONT_VERSION` in `js/fonts.js` (and the `?v=` of the `@font-face` rules of `index.html`, `fonts.html` and `decks/immersive.html` in `qtng/chunom`) is raised when they are built again (v2: component joiner U+F009A, Tham Thư combiner U+F009B, as in the Unicode proposal; v3: the brush tones of Khai and Pen Cut; v4: also of Pen; v5: the lowered marks of tr, x, kh, m (for the tones) in qatt.js, Prototype and Brush too; v6: built from `fonts/paths.json` (the glyph editor); `unicode.html` is generated by `tools/update_unicode_data.py` from `qtng/qatt-unicode`, not edited by hand). The name inside the font files is `Qatt.org-<name>` (e.g. `Qatt.org-Pen Medium`, written by `font_names.py` of the private repository, so it is clear where a font comes from); the CSS names of the pages (`family` in `js/fonts.js`, "QATT Pen Medium") stay, and in the font lists the labels of the Pen fonts start with "Qatt.org ". A change of names only (no new glyphs) does not need a new `FONT_VERSION`.
`apps/notes/` (QATT notes, `https://qatt.org/apps/notes/`) is a notes app as a PWA (installable, offline; in Chrome on Android "Install app"): list, search, editor, autosave (IndexedDB `qatt-notes`, `notes-db.js`, in-memory fallback when it fails), export/import as JSON (`notes-core.js`: titles, search over the QATT text and the readings of its cells, merge by id), fixed dark look (`notes.css`; no themes) and no code of the website's chrome: it does not load `js/navigation.js` but has its own small `lang.js` (EN/VI by `data-vi`, `Lang.t`, switch in the list menu, `localStorage.qattNotesLang`), four fonts of its own (`notes-fonts.js`: Pen medium, Gothic, Gothic wide, Serif; `js/fonts.js` offers Gothic only to admins; `NotesFonts.VERSION` = `FONT_VERSION` of `js/fonts.js`, and the `?v=` of the fonts in `PRECACHE` of `sw.js`). The editing logic is its own file, `notes-editor.js` (`NotesEditor.editor(textarea, { parser, onChange })`: Telex through `ime.js`, QATT at a space, cells whole for cursor, Backspace and Delete, `variants()`, `convert()`, `setDecoding(name)`; `NotesEditor.convertText`). It started as a copy of the behaviour of `js/qatt-writer.js` and is developed independently of it: a change of the website's editor does not reach the app and the other way round. `qatt` and `omniglot` are the tables of `VietnameseParser.DECODINGS` in `js/parser.js`; `new VietnameseParser(name, extra)` also takes more tables by name (`extra`, looked up before `DECODINGS`; the parser has no method that exists only for the app). `decodings.json` holds additional decodings (`[{ name, label, table }]`, now `[]`) which the app loads at every start, checks itself (name, 22 rows of strings; an invalid entry is skipped) and passes as `extra`; an entry with the name of a built-in decoding replaces that table and its label in the app (on purpose: a table can be corrected without an app update; the website keeps reading `parser.js`); without the file only the built-in ones are offered; a decoding that the website should use too goes into `DECODINGS` of `parser.js`. The service worker `apps/notes/sw.js` (`CACHE`, `PRECACHE`): **raise `CACHE` (`qatt-notes-vN`) whenever a file of `PRECACHE` changes** and keep `PRECACHE` equal to what `index.html` loads (a test compares them); `decodings.json` is network first; `ime.js` of chunom.org is cached by the worker (Telex works offline). Tests and the icon generator (`tools/make-icons.mjs`) are in `qtng/qatt-apk` (`node --test --test-timeout=60000 tests/*.test.mjs`, the site from `../qatt` or `QATT_DIR`).
`handwriting.html` (a draft, not in the menu, `noindex`) recognises a QATT cell written by hand while it is written: `js/qatt-ink.js` (`QattInk.create().recognize(strokes)`) takes the templates from the stroke paths of `js/qatt.js` (`defaultSvgDefs`: a letter alone `v2c-<shape>`, a joined cell `v2-<shape>-small` + `v2-<shape>`, the marking strokes `…1`–`…6`, the tones `qt…`; no Tham Thư) and compares the drawing as point clouds in the frame of the cell (chamfer both ways, distance fields), first in the fixed frame, then aligned (move and scale) to each of the best candidates; these are also rated by the kinds of their strokes (direction horizontal, vertical, diagonal; length, so the short marking strokes; round = a tone mark, half or whole circle; the best pairing of the drawn strokes with those of the template, a stroke left over counts fully, `KIND_WEIGHT`). A tone mark is always the last stroke: a candidate with a tone compares it with the last stroke only and its other parts with the strokes before it. The order (apart from the tone) and the direction of the strokes do not matter. The page shows only candidates with a reading.
`handwriting-data.html` (admins only, `noindex`, listed in the admin area) collects handwritten cells to measure and train it: it asks for a cell (of 40 random ones with a reading the one with the fewest samples), the admin writes it on the pad (`js/qatt-pad.js`, `QattPad.mount`, shared with `handwriting.html`; strokes as `{ x, y, t }` in the coordinates of a cell, t in ms) and `ink_submit()` stores it in `ink_samples` (`tools/db/ink.sql`: target code as the results of `QattInk` have it, `strokes` `[[[x, y, t], …], …]`, device; only admins write, nobody reads the table directly, `admin_ink()` gives the counts, the rows are read in the SQL Editor; `ink_undo()` takes the own last sample back).
The SQL of the database (`tools/db/*.sql`, named in the comments and notes here: tables, functions and policies of the Supabase project ChunomApp) and the other build and deployment tools (`tools/qatt/`: `make_vi.py`, `update_unicode_data.py`) are not in this repository but in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private` (`tools/`). The font build (tools), the source data of the letters and tones, the glyph editor, the tone offsets page, the build page and the code table are not in this repository but in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private` (feature `fonts`); `admin/view.html` shows its admin pages to the owner only (`app_metadata.is_owner`, an admin without it sees the rest of the admin area; `view.html?f=fonts&p=admin/glyph-editor.html`; it reads them from the table `private_files`, see `CLAUDE.md` there). The fonts it builds are written to `fonts/` here (`qatt-*.ttf`, `.woff2`, `.marks.json`). `unicode.html` is written by `tools/qatt/update_unicode_data.py` there.
The Edge Function `translate` of the translator of the Chunom site (with its prompts) is not in this repository but in the private repository `qtng/chunom-private` (`supabase/functions/translate/`, `docs/translate.md`); the tables of its log are `tools/db/translate_log.sql` there. What the browser needs stays public; server-side sources and their documentation go to `chunom-private`.

## The sites and their four repositories

Two websites, hosted with GitHub Pages, built from four repositories of the GitHub account `qtng`:

| Repository | Served at | How |
|---|---|---|
| `qtng/qatt` | `https://qatt.org/` (root) | custom domain `qatt.org` set on this repo |
| `qtng/qtng.github.io` | `https://chunom.org/` | the user site; its custom domain is `chunom.org`; the other repos below hang under it as sub-paths |
| `qtng/chunom` | `https://chunom.org/chunom/` | project site of `qtng.github.io` (dictionary, decks, writer, dictionaries) |
| `qtng/pages` | `https://chunom.org/pages/` | project site, published from its folder `docs/` (landing pages of the characters) |
| `qtng/chunom-assets` | `https://chunom.org/chunom-assets/` | project site (images, audio, fonts, scans; no code) |

`chunom`, `pages` and `chunom-assets` are **one site** (the Chunom site, same origin); `qatt` is **a separate site** (another origin).
GitHub Pages allows one custom domain per repository, which is why `qatt` has its own domain and the others share `qtng.github.io`.

## URL rules (important when you add or change a link)

- **Inside the Chunom site** (`chunom`, `pages`, `chunom-assets`) use **root-relative** paths: `/chunom/js/detail.js`, `/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`, `/pages/site.js`. Never write the domain, so the same file works on any domain of the site.
- **From the Chunom site to qatt.org** use **absolute** URLs: `https://qatt.org/vendor/bootstrap/…` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase), `https://qatt.org/js/qatt.js`, `parser.js`, `supabase.js`, `login.js`, `https://qatt.org/fonts/…`.
- **Inside qatt.org** use **root paths** without any folder prefix: `/js/navigation.js`, `/editor.html`, `/img/logo.svg` (the old prefix `/qatt/` no longer exists).
- **From qatt.org to the Chunom site** use **absolute** URLs with the domain: `https://chunom.org/chunom/js/ime.js`, `https://chunom.org/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`.
- **Scripts that other pages load across sites** (`chunom/js/detail.js`, `ime.js`, `practice.js`) must not assume their own origin: they derive their location from `document.currentScript.src` (e.g. `new URL('../../', BASE)` = root of the Chunom site).
- Canonical links, `og:` tags and the sitemap need absolute URLs; `qtng/pages` writes them from `tools/pages/generate.py --site …` of the private repository `qtng/chunom-private`.
- There is **one `js/navigation.js`** (menu bar, brand, bottom bar on phones, and the translation `Lang`), in `qtng/qatt`, served at `https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js`. The pages of the Chunom site load it from there with `<script src="https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js"></script>` early in `<head>` (a normal script, not `defer`, so that the page is translated while it is parsed). It picks brand, logo, icons, colours and the own block of the menu from the address of the page (`chunom.org` or the folders `/chunom/`, `/pages/`, `/chunom-assets/` = Chunom site, else qatt.org); the constants `CHUNOM_SITE` and `QATT_SITE` hold the absolute addresses for the links to the other site. The Chunom logo and icons are in `chunom/img/`.
- The Supabase login (decks, practice) is stored in `localStorage`, i.e. **per origin**: a user signed in on `qatt.org` is not signed in on the Chunom site.
- The site is plain HTML and JavaScript with Bootstrap 5.3, no build step. Local preview of the real URLs is not possible; to test, serve the repos under mocked hosts (Playwright `page.route` mapping `https://chunom.org/chunom/*`, `/chunom-assets/*`, `/pages/*` and `https://qatt.org/*` to the checkouts) and look for 404s and console errors.

## Translation (Vietnamese)

`js/navigation.js` defines `Lang` (also on the Chunom site). The language is `localStorage.lang` (`en` or `vi`) if somebody chose it (the switch EN | VI next to the menu button in the menu bar, also in `license.html` of qatt.org, or `?lang=vi` in the address), else the first of English and Vietnamese in the languages of the browser (`vi` only if it comes before `en`). It is not stored until it was chosen.
In `vi`, a MutationObserver gives every tag with `data-vi` the text of that attribute as its text content (the English text is kept and returns in `en`). It works while the page is parsed and for tags that JavaScript adds or changes later.

Rules when you write or change pages:

- A text that is translated sits **in a tag of its own without tags inside**: `<span data-vi="Ký hiệu">Symbols</span>`. Tags with tags inside are not translated. The English text stays in the tag and must not be empty.
- When the user says that something is called `xyz` in Vietnamese, put `data-vi="xyz"` on the tag of that text (wrap the text in a `<span>` if it has no tag of its own).
- When the English text of a tag that has `data-vi` changes, renew its translation too and tell the user which ones (translations written by Claude are drafts: say so).
- Texts are written in sentence case, not title case ("Symbol table", "Next word"); only app names such as Spell it! are capitalised.
- Scope: translate interface elements (buttons, menu entries, labels, option groups, placeholders) and headings. Long texts (paragraphs, explanations) stay English; single English words in a Vietnamese interface are fine, a completely foreign interface is not.
- In JavaScript just set the text of such a tag (or add one with `data-vi`); the observer translates it. `Lang.t("English", "Tiếng Việt")` gives a text for a string that is not in a tag; the event `langchange` (on `document`) is sent when the language is switched. Attributes: `data-vi-label`, `data-vi-title`, `data-vi-placeholder` and `data-vi-aria-label` give the attribute without the prefix its Vietnamese text (e.g. the `label` of an `<optgroup>`: `<optgroup label="Choose font" data-vi-label="Lựa chọn phông chữ">`).

## Going live on chunom.org (done)

`chunom.org` is the address of the Chunom site (done, with `v2.chunom.org` before). What was done, for reference or for a move to another domain:

1. DNS of `chunom.org` to GitHub Pages (A records `185.199.108.153` … `185.199.111.153`; `www` as CNAME to `qtng.github.io`), then the custom domain `chunom.org` in `qtng.github.io` → Settings → Pages and enforce HTTPS. Never use "Unpublish site" to switch domains (it removes the certificate).
2. In `qtng/qatt` the domain in `js/navigation.js` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `404.html` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `spell.html`, `editor.html`, `admin/index.html` and the default `PROMPTS_URL` / allowed origins of the function `translate`.
3. In `qtng/chunom-private` `python3 tools/pages/generate.py --site https://chunom.org` (add `--chunom ../chunom --assets ../chunom-assets` for local checkouts; it writes the checkout of `qtng/pages` next to it) and commit `docs/` in `qtng/pages`.
4. Nothing to change in `qtng/chunom` and `qtng/chunom-assets` (root-relative paths).
5. Search Console: verify `chunom.org`, submit `https://chunom.org/pages/sitemap.xml`.
