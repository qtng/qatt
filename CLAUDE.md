# qtng/qatt

The QATT site (Quốc Âm Tân Tự, a script for Vietnamese): `index.html`, `symbols.html`, `editor.html`, `spell.html` ("Spell it!", vi "Đánh vần!", the name of the game; `check.html` and `soundcheck.html` only forward to it: spell a word: onset/rhyme shapes, then the tone; the words are the one-syllable ranked entries of `chunom/dict.csv`, loaded from the Chunom site; score: +10 a right choice, −2 a wrong one, a bonus for a solved word that falls from 50 to 0 in the minute, a cheer and +25 for a quick word; the speed of the player per kind of symbol (onset, rhyme, tone; an onset or rhyme cell is one item, its time the mean of the clicks of its shape and its marked form; a wrong click on a shape or a marked form goes to the kind of the next right click) as exponentially smoothed times (slow `ALPHA` 0.05 for the server and the ranking, fast `ALPHA_FAST` 0.1 for the display and +50 at a milestone such as onsets under 2 s; a time counts at most as the own slow value + 2 s (at most 10 s; 10 s until `MIN_SAMPLES`); a wrong choice counts as the own time + 2 s; the slow and the fast value stop at 10 s; the hint of the right button comes after the own value of that kind + 5 s without a click (5 s until `MIN_SAMPLES`), a step with a hint does not count in the speed; from `MIN_SAMPLES` = 5 times of a kind they count: quick words, milestones, later the ranking); Skip: the word does not count at all (score and times as before the word), then Skip is locked until a word is solved whose points beat the smoothed points of the words so far (`avg`); for a signed-in player (not an anonymous one; `SupabaseService.initQuiet`, no anonymous account is made) the points and the speed go to the server (`db/spell.sql`: `spell_scores`, `spell_speed`, `spell_submit`, `spell_leaderboard`, project ChunomApp), and the ranking under the game reads from there (shown only to admins for now, `RANKING_PUBLIC` in the page); every 5 solved words a card shows the score and a gauge "Confidence" (per kind of symbol the target 1 s against the slow time, mean of the kinds with at least `MIN_SAMPLES` times; constants `STATS_EVERY`, `TARGET`); a visitor sees the points rise but the score stays 0 (they are kept in `shadow`), is asked to sign in at 60, 300 and 1000 points with a "Not now" button, and nothing is taken over at the first sign-in; the browser keeps (`localStorage.qattSpell`: `{ score, decoding, stats: { <decoding>: { onset, rhyme, tone } } }`, the times kept per decoding) so far, constants `POINTS`, `CAP`, `ALPHA` in the page), `decodings.html`, `unicode.html`, `wiki.html`, `practice/`, `vandao/`, `admin/`, with `js/`, `vendor/` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase, shared by both sites), `fonts/`, `img/`.
Served at the **root** of `qatt.org`. `js/navigation.js` is the menu (and the translation `Lang`) of both sites; the pages of the Chunom site load it from here. `404.html` forwards old `/qatt/…` addresses to the same path without the prefix and old `/chunom/`, `/chunom-assets/`, `/pages/` addresses to the Chunom site (prefix kept); the countdown is 2 seconds.
`css/site.css` is what all pages share whatever the theme (colour variables, the padding of `btn-pill`); load it right after Bootstrap (`<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/site.css">`, the path as for `vendor/`). The look of buttons (`btn-pill`, `btn-cinnabar`, `btn-accent`, `btn-quiet`, `btn-ink`), dropdowns and the other shared elements is in a **theme file**, see below. Bootstrap draws buttons from `--bs-btn-*` variables, so set those (not `border-color`) to change the colours of a button. The pages of the Chunom site under `/chunom/` link it too (`https://qatt.org/css/site.css`).
Buttons never wrap (`.btn { white-space: nowrap }` in `css/site.css`), also in Vietnamese.
For admins (`app_metadata.is_admin`, as for the palette button) the menu bar also shows a shield icon (`.admin-link`) that links to `https://qatt.org/admin/`.
`js/navigation.js` also adds a round floating button (`.to-top`, in the colour of the bar `--nav-bg`, an arrow up) that scrolls to the top after 400px of scrolling; on the phone it sits above the bottom bar.
Themes (qatt.org and the Chunom site): `localStorage.theme1` (the key was `theme` before; a new key makes earlier choices void; chosen with the palette button next to EN | VI in the menu bar (shown only to admins, `app_metadata.is_admin` in the session of the browser, on both sites; the session is per site); `license.html` has no theme choice; `navigation.js` takes `?theme=` up, stores it where themes can be chosen, otherwise only shows it on that page, and removes it from the address; `classic`; `retro`; `popart`; `editorial`, a printed magazine: serif on warm paper, thin rules, spaced capitals on the buttons; `swiss`, the International Typographic Style: grotesque, black on white, one red, flush-left headers, heavy rules; `glass` (glassmorphism), `clay` (claymorphism), `boho` (bohemian, the default of both sites for visitors who have not chosen a theme), `pixel` (pixel art), `vector` (flat vector illustration), `neobrutal` (neobrutalism with colours), `vietnam` (modern Vietnam: lacquer red and gold, lotus pink, giấy dó paper, a lattice pattern), `future` (futuristic, the only dark theme: it makes `--ink` light and darkens the light boxes the pages draw of their own, e.g. the cells and game buttons of Spell it!, so check a dark theme on every page; it also turns the black images of Nôm characters of the Chunom site (`/chunom-assets/generated/`, the animations and glyph SVGs of the detail popup) light and gives dropdown entries, the close button of dialogs and `.form-text` light colours); chosen with the palette button) says which file `css/theme-<name>.css` `Theme` in `js/navigation.js` writes into the `<head>` (`document.write`, so the page is not drawn without it, which is why `navigation.js` is a normal script, not `defer`, on the pages of qatt.org) and sets `data-theme` on `<html>`. **Every theme is a complete file of its own; none builds on, overrides or needs another.** `theme-classic.css` is the look before the retro redesign (pill buttons with a thin soft outline, the look of the Chunom site), `theme-retro.css` the retro look (a 2px dark outline, a hard shadow, softly rounded corners), `theme-popart.css` is the retro look (a copy, keep it in step) plus its own rules under `html[data-theme="popart"]`, which are more specific than the colour variables and rules of the pages' own `<style>`. Pages keep only their layout in their own `<style>`: colours, outlines, shadows and corners of shared elements (buttons, dropdowns, the symbol buttons of `symbols.html`, the search bar of `wiki.html`, the controls of `decodings.html`) belong in the theme files, in each one. A new theme is a new file, an entry in `Theme.list` . Each theme also gives the menu bar and the bottom bar of the phone (`js/navigation.js`) a dark tint of its own: `--nav-bg` (the bar and its menus), optionally `--nav-bottom-bg` (the bottom bar of the phone is dark grey with a light touch (22%) of `--nav-bg` by default), `--nav-alpha` (how opaque, default 95%) and `--nav-line` (the edge); `classic` keeps the grey. The Chunom logo is a PNG without a background (`chunom/img/chunom-logo.png`), so the tint shows around it. Pages without `navigation.js` (`decoding.html`, `vandao/`, `admin/rhymes.html`) link `css/theme-classic.css` themselves. The pages of the Chunom site under `/chunom/` load the theme too, from `https://qatt.org/css/…`; they have the skeleton of qatt.org (`.hero`, `.card-box`, the button classes; layout only in their own `<style>`), so the theme can be chosen there too, with the palette button (`CHUNOM_THEMES` in `Theme`; `false` would keep them `classic`); the choice is stored per site (localStorage is per origin), so a visitor can have different themes on the two sites. A change to a theme file therefore needs a look at the Chunom pages too (search, translate, writer, decks, practice, dictionaries, the detail popup). The landing pages (`/pages/`) and the assets load no theme. Spell it! (`spell.html`) keeps its own retro rules for its game buttons in every theme. Test each theme on all pages (screenshots of index, symbols, spell, decodings, unicode, wiki, codetable, fonts, type, editor) and compare with the previous state pixel by pixel when you restructure.
`js/navigation.js` also counts the visits of both sites (`db/visits.sql`): numbers only, on the first tap/key/wheel of a page, not for bots; `localStorage.qv_off = "1"` leaves a browser out. The admin area (`admin/`, `db/admin_stats.sql`, `db/stats_daily.sql`, `db/translate_log.sql`) shows the numbers; who is an admin is `app_metadata.is_admin` of the user, checked in the database.
Docs of the tools: `tools/README.md`.

## The sites and their four repositories

Two websites, hosted with GitHub Pages, built from four repositories of the GitHub account `qtng`:

| Repository | Served at | How |
|---|---|---|
| `qtng/qatt` | `https://qatt.org/` (root) | custom domain `qatt.org` set on this repo |
| `qtng/qtng.github.io` | `https://chunom.org/` | the user site; its custom domain is `chunom.org`; the other repos below hang under it as sub-paths |
| `qtng/chunom` | `https://chunom.org/chunom/` | project site of `qtng.github.io` (dictionary, decks, writer, dictionaries) |
| `qtng/pages` | `https://chunom.org/pages/` | project site, published from its folder `docs/` (landing pages of the characters) |

`chunom`, `pages` and `chunom-assets` are **one site** (the Chunom site, same origin); `qatt` is **a separate site** (another origin).
GitHub Pages allows one custom domain per repository, which is why `qatt` has its own domain and the others share `qtng.github.io`.
The Chunom site is live on `chunom.org` (the former test address `v2.chunom.org` is no longer used; see "Going live" for how it was done).

## URL rules (important when you add or change a link)

- **Inside the Chunom site** (`chunom`, `pages`, `chunom-assets`) use **root-relative** paths: `/chunom/js/detail.js`, `/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`, `/pages/site.js`. Never write the domain, so the same file works on any domain of the site.
- **From the Chunom site to qatt.org** use **absolute** URLs: `https://qatt.org/vendor/bootstrap/…` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase), `https://qatt.org/js/qatt.js`, `parser.js`, `supabase.js`, `login.js`, `https://qatt.org/fonts/…`.
- **Inside qatt.org** use **root paths** without any folder prefix: `/js/navigation.js`, `/editor.html`, `/img/logo.svg` (the old prefix `/qatt/` no longer exists).
- **From qatt.org to the Chunom site** use **absolute** URLs with the domain: `https://chunom.org/chunom/js/ime.js`, `https://chunom.org/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`.
- **Scripts that other pages load across sites** (`chunom/js/detail.js`, `ime.js`, `practice.js`) must not assume their own origin: they derive their location from `document.currentScript.src` (e.g. `new URL('../../', BASE)` = root of the Chunom site).
- Canonical links, `og:` tags and the sitemap need absolute URLs; `qtng/pages` writes them from `tools/generate.py --site …`.
- There is **one `js/navigation.js`** (menu bar, brand, bottom bar on phones, and the translation `Lang`), in `qtng/qatt`, served at `https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js`. The pages of the Chunom site load it from there with `<script src="https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js"></script>` early in `<head>` (a normal script, not `defer`, so that the page is translated while it is parsed). It picks brand, logo, icons, colours and the own block of the menu from the address of the page (`chunom.org` or the folders `/chunom/`, `/pages/`, `/chunom-assets/` = Chunom site, else qatt.org); the constants `CHUNOM_SITE` and `QATT_SITE` hold the absolute addresses for the links to the other site. The Chunom logo and icons are in `chunom/img/`.
- The Supabase login (decks, practice) is stored in `localStorage`, i.e. **per origin**: a user signed in on `qatt.org` is not signed in on the Chunom site.
- The site is plain HTML and JavaScript with Bootstrap 5.3, no build step. Local preview of the real URLs is not possible; to test, serve the repos under mocked hosts (Playwright `page.route` mapping `https://chunom.org/chunom/*`, `/chunom-assets/*`, `/pages/*` and `https://qatt.org/*` to the checkouts) and look for 404s and console errors.

## Translation (Vietnamese)

`js/navigation.js` defines `Lang` (also on the Chunom site). The language is `localStorage.lang` (`en` or `vi`) if somebody chose it (the switch EN | VI next to the menu button in the menu bar, also in `license.html` of qatt.org, or `?lang=vi` in the address), else the first of English and Vietnamese in the languages of the browser (`vi` only if it comes before `en`). It is not stored until it was chosen.
In `vi`, a MutationObserver gives every tag with `data-vi` the text of that attribute as its text content (the English text is kept and returns in `en`). It works while the page is parsed and for tags that JavaScript adds or changes later.

Rules when you write or change pages:

- A text that is translated sits **in a tag of its own without tags inside**: `<span data-vi="Ký hiệu">Symbols</span>`. Tags with tags inside are not translated. The English text stays in the tag and must not be empty.
- When the user says that something is called `xyz` in Vietnamese, put `data-vi="xyz"` on the tag of that text (wrap the text in a `<span>` if it has no tag of its own).
- When the English text of a tag that has `data-vi` changes, renew its translation too and tell the user which ones (translations written by Claude are drafts: say so).
- Texts are written in sentence case, not title case ("Symbol table", "Next word"); only app names such as Spell it! are capitalised.
- Scope: translate interface elements (buttons, menu entries, labels, option groups, placeholders) and headings. Long texts (paragraphs, explanations) stay English; single English words in a Vietnamese interface are fine, a completely foreign interface is not.
- In JavaScript just set the text of such a tag (or add one with `data-vi`); the observer translates it. `Lang.t("English", "Tiếng Việt")` gives a text for a string that is not in a tag; the event `langchange` (on `document`) is sent when the language is switched. Attributes: `data-vi-label`, `data-vi-title`, `data-vi-placeholder` and `data-vi-aria-label` give the attribute without the prefix its Vietnamese text (e.g. the `label` of an `<optgroup>`: `<optgroup label="Choose font" data-vi-label="Lựa chọn phông chữ">`).

## Going live on chunom.org (done)

`chunom.org` is the address of the Chunom site (done, with `v2.chunom.org` before). What was done, for reference or for a move to another domain:

1. DNS of `chunom.org` to GitHub Pages (A records `185.199.108.153` … `185.199.111.153`; `www` as CNAME to `qtng.github.io`), then the custom domain `chunom.org` in `qtng.github.io` → Settings → Pages and enforce HTTPS. Never use "Unpublish site" to switch domains (it removes the certificate).
2. In `qtng/qatt` the domain in `js/navigation.js` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `404.html` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `spell.html`, `editor.html`, `type.html`, `practice/qatt.html`, `admin/index.html` and the default `PROMPTS_URL` / allowed origins of the function `translate`.
3. In `qtng/pages` `python3 tools/generate.py --site https://chunom.org` (add `--chunom ../chunom --assets ../chunom-assets` for local checkouts) and commit `docs/`.
4. Nothing to change in `qtng/chunom` and `qtng/chunom-assets` (root-relative paths).
5. Search Console: verify `chunom.org`, submit `https://chunom.org/pages/sitemap.xml`.
