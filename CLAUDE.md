# qtng/qatt

The QATT site (Quốc Âm Tân Tự, a script for Vietnamese): `index.html`, `symbols.html`, `editor.html`, `spell.html` ("Spell it!", vi "Đánh vần!", the name of the game: spell a word: onset and rhyme shapes together on 6 buttons in two rows of 3 (the 22 base shapes are in rows of 8, 8 and 6 in the order of `symbols.html` (`ROWS`); the rows of the onset and of the rhyme are the two rows of the word, one row alone gets the next one (the one above for the last); each is filled up to 3 shapes at random, in the order of the row), then the tone; the words are the one-syllable ranked entries of `chunom/dict.csv`, loaded from the Chunom site; score: +10 a right choice, −2 a wrong one, a bonus for a solved word that falls from 50 to 0 in the minute, a cheer and +25 for a quick word; the speed of the player per kind of symbol (onset, rhyme, tone; an onset or rhyme cell is one item, its time the mean of the clicks of its shape and its marked form; a wrong click on a shape or a marked form goes to the kind of the next right click) as exponentially smoothed times (slow `ALPHA` 0.05 for the server and the ranking, faster `ALPHA_FAST` 0.2 (after 10 equal times about 90% of the way) for the display, the card and +50 at a milestone such as onsets under 2 s; a time counts at most as the own slow value + 2 s (at most 10 s; 10 s until `MIN_SAMPLES`); a wrong choice counts as the own time + 2 s; the slow and the fast value stop at 10 s; the hint of the right button comes after the own value of that kind + 5 s without a click (5 s until `MIN_SAMPLES`), a step with a hint does not count in the speed; from `MIN_SAMPLES` = 5 times of a kind they count: quick words, milestones, later the ranking); Skip: the word does not count at all (score and times as before the word), then Skip is locked until a word is solved whose points beat the smoothed points of the words so far (`avg`); for a signed-in player (not an anonymous one; `SupabaseService.initQuiet`, no anonymous account is made) the points and the speed go to the server (`db/spell.sql`: `spell_scores`, `spell_speed`, `spell_submit`, `spell_leaderboard`, project ChunomApp), and the ranking under the game reads from there (shown only to admins for now, `RANKING_PUBLIC` in the page); every 5 solved words (`STATS_EVERY`) a card shows the score and, for those last words only (the short time, no long-term value), stars per kind of symbol: the mean of the times since the last card (a wrong choice is a slow time) as the status line shows them, a star for each time of `MILESTONES` it beats (0 to 5), the mean time, an arrow ↑ ↓ against the card before and ✗n for the wrong choices; the gauge (needle, "Form") over them is the mean of the kinds on the same scale with the parts between two times, 0 to 100; the card rewards: the stars light up one after the other (the new ones against the card before glow), the number runs up with the needle, sparks by the result (green many, yellow a few, red none; not with reduced motion), a badge "New best form!" (`me.best`) and the gain since the last card (`me.lastValue`), and a line with the best kind of the round (always positive) (`me.round`, `me.prev`; a skipped word takes its times back); at the bottom of the page a log (stopwatch): the first line is the word in play with the time of every click (✗ for a wrong one) and the running one, the lines below the earlier words, to check the measuring; the status line also shows the last time of every kind after an arrow; what is shown (status line, card, gauge, milestones) takes `CLICK_TIME` off every kind (the time to find and press a button: 0.5 s, for a tone 1 s because its 8 marks, 4 circles and 4 half circles, differ only by their place; a rhyme is the mean of its clicks, so it is the same off the mean) and `READ_TIME` 1.25 s more off the onset (1.75 s in all) (the time to read the word is in its first click; at least `MIN_SHOWN` 0.1 s), what is kept, sent to the server and shown in the stopwatch is the measured time; a visitor sees the points rise but the score stays 0 (they are kept in `shadow`), is asked to sign in at 60, 300 and 1000 points with a "Not now" button, and nothing is taken over at the first sign-in; the browser keeps (`localStorage.qattSpell`: `{ score, decoding, stats: { <decoding>: { onset, rhyme, tone } } }`, the times kept per decoding) so far, constants `POINTS`, `CAP`, `ALPHA` in the page), `decodings.html`, `unicode.html`, `wiki.html`, `vandao/`, `admin/`, with `js/`, `vendor/` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase, shared by both sites), `fonts/`, `img/`.
Served at the **root** of `qatt.org`. `js/navigation.js` is the menu (and the translation `Lang`) of both sites; the pages of the Chunom site load it from here. `404.html` forwards old `/qatt/…` addresses to the same path without the prefix and old `/chunom/`, `/chunom-assets/`, `/pages/` addresses to the Chunom site (prefix kept); the countdown is 2 seconds; the old `/practice/` and `/app.html` (the former practice of the script, removed) go to `spell.html`.
`css/site.css` is what all pages share whatever the theme (colour variables, the padding of `btn-pill`); load it right after Bootstrap (`<link rel="stylesheet" href="css/site.css">`, the path as for `vendor/`). The look of buttons (`btn-pill`, `btn-cinnabar`, `btn-accent`, `btn-quiet`, `btn-ink`), dropdowns and the other shared elements is in a **theme file**, see below. Bootstrap draws buttons from `--bs-btn-*` variables, so set those (not `border-color`) to change the colours of a button. The pages of the Chunom site under `/chunom/` link it too (`https://qatt.org/css/site.css`).
Buttons never wrap (`.btn { white-space: nowrap }` in `css/site.css`), also in Vietnamese.
For admins (`app_metadata.is_admin`, as for the palette button) the menu bar also shows a shield icon (`.admin-link`) that links to `https://qatt.org/admin/`.
`js/navigation.js` also adds a round floating button (`.to-top`, in the colour of the bar `--nav-bg`, an arrow up) that scrolls to the top after 400px of scrolling; on the phone it sits above the bottom bar.
Themes (qatt.org and the Chunom site): `localStorage.theme1` (the key was `theme` before; a new key makes earlier choices void; chosen with the palette button next to EN | VI in the menu bar (shown only to admins, `app_metadata.is_admin` in the session of the browser, on both sites; the session is per site); `license.html` has no theme choice; `navigation.js` takes `?theme=` up, stores it where themes can be chosen, otherwise only shows it on that page, and removes it from the address; `classic`; `retro`; `popart`; `editorial`, a printed magazine: serif on warm paper, thin rules, spaced capitals on the buttons; `swiss`, the International Typographic Style: grotesque, black on white, one red, flush-left headers, heavy rules; `glass` (glassmorphism), `clay` (claymorphism), `boho` (bohemian, the default of both sites for visitors who have not chosen a theme), `pixel` (pixel art), `vector` (flat vector illustration), `neobrutal` (neobrutalism with colours), `vietnam` (modern Vietnam: lacquer red and gold, lotus pink, giấy dó paper, a lattice pattern), `future` (futuristic, the only dark theme: it makes `--ink` light and darkens the light boxes the pages draw of their own, e.g. the cells and game buttons of Spell it!, so check a dark theme on every page; it also turns the black images of Nôm characters of the Chunom site (`/chunom-assets/generated/`, the animations and glyph SVGs of the detail popup) light and gives dropdown entries, the close button of dialogs and `.form-text` light colours); chosen with the palette button) says which file `css/theme-<name>.css` `Theme` in `js/navigation.js` writes into the `<head>` (`document.write`, so the page is not drawn without it, which is why `navigation.js` is a normal script, not `defer`, on the pages of qatt.org) and sets `data-theme` on `<html>`. **Every theme is a complete file of its own; none builds on, overrides or needs another.** `theme-classic.css` is the look before the retro redesign (pill buttons with a thin soft outline, the look of the Chunom site), `theme-retro.css` the retro look (a 2px dark outline, a hard shadow, softly rounded corners), `theme-popart.css` is the retro look (a copy, keep it in step) plus its own rules under `html[data-theme="popart"]`, which are more specific than the colour variables and rules of the pages' own `<style>`. Pages keep only their layout in their own `<style>`: colours, outlines, shadows and corners of shared elements (buttons, dropdowns, the symbol buttons of `symbols.html`, the search bar of `wiki.html`, the controls of `decodings.html`) belong in the theme files, in each one. A new theme is a new file, an entry in `Theme.list` . Each theme also gives the menu bar and the bottom bar of the phone (`js/navigation.js`) a dark tint of its own: `--nav-bg` (the bar and its menus), optionally `--nav-bottom-bg` (the bottom bar of the phone is dark grey with a light touch (22%) of `--nav-bg` by default), `--nav-alpha` (how opaque, default 95%) and `--nav-line` (the edge); `classic` keeps the grey. The Chunom logo is a PNG without a background (`chunom/img/chunom-logo.png`), so the tint shows around it. Pages without `navigation.js` (`admin/decoding.html`, `vandao/`, `admin/rhymes.html`) link `css/theme-classic.css` themselves. The pages of the Chunom site under `/chunom/` load the theme too, from `https://qatt.org/css/…`; they have the skeleton of qatt.org (`.hero`, `.card-box`, the button classes; layout only in their own `<style>`), so the theme can be chosen there too, with the palette button (`CHUNOM_THEMES` in `Theme`; `false` would keep them `classic`); the choice is stored per site (localStorage is per origin), so a visitor can have different themes on the two sites. A change to a theme file therefore needs a look at the Chunom pages too (search, translate, writer, decks, practice, dictionaries, the detail popup). The landing pages (`/pages/`) and the assets load no theme. Spell it! (`spell.html`) keeps its own retro rules for its game buttons in every theme. Test each theme on all pages (screenshots of index, symbols, spell, decodings, unicode, wiki, admin/font/codetable, fonts, type, editor) and compare with the previous state pixel by pixel when you restructure.
`js/navigation.js` also counts the visits of both sites (`db/visits.sql`): numbers only, on the first tap/key/wheel of a page, not for bots; `localStorage.qv_off = "1"` leaves a browser out. The admin area (`admin/`, `db/admin_stats.sql`, `db/admin_users.sql` for the list of accounts in `admin/users.html`, `db/stats_daily.sql`, `db/translate_log.sql`, `admin_spell()` in `db/spell.sql` for the players and words per day of Spell it!) shows the numbers; who is an admin is `app_metadata.is_admin` of the user, checked in the database.
The editor of `editor.html` and of `writer.html` of the Chunom site is one: `js/qatt-writer.js` (`QattWriter.mount(element, { switch, storage })`, loaded by both pages from qatt.org; the QATT toolbar above the card (decoding, font, Convert QATT, 品 = Tham Thư: stacks the two characters before the cursor by putting a combiner U+F009A between them, enabled only when the cursor is behind two letters U+F0000..U+F0099 whose second is a single one (tone marks behind it may stand) and the first is not the upper part of a stack; centred, only while QATT is on, its space stays so the card does not move; one row, never wraps), the size in the card, the field, Telex, vertical text, Example and the cells kept whole; Convert reads Tham Thư apart, as if the combiner were not there: the parts of a stack are read one by one and the combiner becomes the IDS character ⿱ (U+2FF1) between the readings, and ⿱ between QATT cells becomes the combiner again; the 品 button keeps the focus in the field; needs `parser.js`, `qatt-text.js`, `fonts.js` and `ime.js` of the Chunom site). The pages keep only header, card colours and the text around. The difference is the switch: `switch: 'nom'` (qatt.org: QATT always on, "Chữ Nôm" turns the candidate list on, off at the start) and `switch: 'qatt'` (Chunom: candidates always on, "Quốc Âm Tân Tự" turns QATT on, off at the start). A change to the editor is made there, once, and needs a look at both pages (and in the themes). Both pages load it with `?v=N` (`editor.html` here, `writer.html` in `qtng/chunom`): raise N in both with every change of `js/qatt-writer.js`, the file is cached for 4 hours (GitHub Pages `max-age=14400`), and a visitor of chunom.org cannot refresh a file of another domain.
Docs of the tools: `tools/README.md`.

## URL rules (important when you add or change a link)

- **Inside the Chunom site** (`chunom`, `pages`, `chunom-assets`) use **root-relative** paths: `/chunom/js/detail.js`, `/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`, `/pages/site.js`. Never write the domain, so the same file works on any domain of the site.
- **From the Chunom site to qatt.org** use **absolute** URLs: `https://qatt.org/vendor/bootstrap/…` (Bootstrap, Bootstrap Icons, Supabase), `https://qatt.org/js/qatt.js`, `parser.js`, `supabase.js`, `login.js`, `https://qatt.org/fonts/…`.
- **Inside qatt.org** use **root paths** without any folder prefix: `/js/navigation.js`, `/editor.html`, `/img/logo.svg` (the old prefix `/qatt/` no longer exists).
- **From qatt.org to the Chunom site** use **absolute** URLs with the domain: `https://chunom.org/chunom/js/ime.js`, `https://chunom.org/chunom-assets/fonts/fonts.css`.
- **Scripts that other pages load across sites** (`chunom/js/detail.js`, `ime.js`, `practice.js`) must not assume their own origin: they derive their location from `document.currentScript.src` (e.g. `new URL('../../', BASE)` = root of the Chunom site).
- Canonical links, `og:` tags and the sitemap need absolute URLs; `qtng/pages` writes them from `tools/generate.py --site …`.
- There is **one `js/navigation.js`** (menu bar, brand, bottom bar on phones, and the translation `Lang`), in `qtng/qatt`, served at `https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js`. The pages of the Chunom site load it from there with `<script src="https://qatt.org/js/navigation.js"></script>` early in `<head>` (a normal script, not `defer`, so that the page is translated while it is parsed). It picks brand, logo, icons, colours and the own block of the menu from the address of the page (`chunom.org` or the folders `/chunom/`, `/pages/`, `/chunom-assets/` = Chunom site, else qatt.org); the constants `CHUNOM_SITE` and `QATT_SITE` hold the absolute addresses for the links to the other site. The Chunom logo and icons are in `chunom/img/`.
- The Supabase login (decks, practice) is stored in `localStorage`, i.e. **per origin**: a user signed in on `qatt.org` is not signed in on the Chunom site.
- The site is plain HTML and JavaScript with Bootstrap 5.3, no build step. Local preview of the real URLs is not possible; to test, serve the repos under mocked hosts (Playwright `page.route` mapping `https://chunom.org/chunom/*`, `/chunom-assets/*`, `/pages/*` and `https://qatt.org/*` to the checkouts) and look for 404s and console errors.

## Translation (Vietnamese)

`js/navigation.js` defines `Lang` (also on the Chunom site). The language is `localStorage.lang` (`en` or `vi`) if somebody chose it (the switch EN | VI next to the menu button in the menu bar, also in `license.html` of qatt.org, or `?lang=vi` in the address), else the first of English and Vietnamese in the languages of the browser (`vi` only if it comes before `en`). It is not stored until it was chosen.
In `vi`, a MutationObserver gives every tag with `data-vi` the text of that attribute as its text content (the English text is kept and returns in `en`). It works while the page is parsed and for tags that JavaScript adds or changes later.

Rules when you write or change pages:

- A text that is translated sits **in a tag of its own without tags inside**: `<span data-vi="Ký hiệu">Symbols</span>`. Tags with tags inside are not translated. The English text stays in the tag and must not be empty.
- When the user says that something is called `xyz` in Vietnamese, put `data-vi="xyz"` on the tag of that text (wrap the text in a `<span>` if it has no tag of its own).
- When the English text of a tag that has `data-vi` changes, renew its translation too and tell the user which ones (translations written by Claude are drafts: say so).
- Texts are written in sentence case, not title case ("Symbol table", "Next word"); only app names such as Spell it! are capitalised.
- Scope: translate interface elements (buttons, menu entries, labels, option groups, placeholders) and headings. Long texts (paragraphs, explanations) stay English; single English words in a Vietnamese interface are fine, a completely foreign interface is not.
- In JavaScript just set the text of such a tag (or add one with `data-vi`); the observer translates it. `Lang.t("English", "Tiếng Việt")` gives a text for a string that is not in a tag; the event `langchange` (on `document`) is sent when the language is switched. Attributes: `data-vi-label`, `data-vi-title`, `data-vi-placeholder` and `data-vi-aria-label` give the attribute without the prefix its Vietnamese text (e.g. the `label` of an `<optgroup>`: `<optgroup label="Choose font" data-vi-label="Lựa chọn phông chữ">`).

## Going live on chunom.org (done)

`chunom.org` is the address of the Chunom site (done, with `v2.chunom.org` before). What was done, for reference or for a move to another domain:

1. DNS of `chunom.org` to GitHub Pages (A records `185.199.108.153` … `185.199.111.153`; `www` as CNAME to `qtng.github.io`), then the custom domain `chunom.org` in `qtng.github.io` → Settings → Pages and enforce HTTPS. Never use "Unpublish site" to switch domains (it removes the certificate).
2. In `qtng/qatt` the domain in `js/navigation.js` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `404.html` constant `CHUNOM_SITE`, `spell.html`, `editor.html`, `admin/font/qatt-editor.html`, `admin/index.html` and the default `PROMPTS_URL` / allowed origins of the function `translate`.
3. In `qtng/pages` `python3 tools/generate.py --site https://chunom.org` (add `--chunom ../chunom --assets ../chunom-assets` for local checkouts) and commit `docs/`.
4. Nothing to change in `qtng/chunom` and `qtng/chunom-assets` (root-relative paths).
5. Search Console: verify `chunom.org`, submit `https://chunom.org/pages/sitemap.xml`.
