Rhymes

Marking strokes 2 to 6 make a shape a rhyme.

A marking stroke turns a base shape into a rhyme. Each of the strokes 2 to 6 gives one column of the rhyme table: 22 shapes × 5 strokes = 110 rhymes. A marked shape that stands alone is read with its own shape as the initial, so the rows read lơ, nga, ghe …. Stroke 1 is special: see the lesson Hợp Thư.
