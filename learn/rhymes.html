Rhymes

Marking strokes 1 to 6 make a shape a rhyme.

A marking stroke turns a base shape into a rhyme. The shapes come in 11 pairs, the second the mirror image of the first: every tab is one pair, its rows are the strokes 1 to 6 on both (the last tab is the first pair once more, named after its second shape). Stroke 1 always gives the rhyme ông; with the strokes 2 to 6, each of the 22 shapes gives five rhymes, 110 in all. A marked shape that stands alone is read with its own shape as the initial, so the rows read lông, lơ, … More about ông and the rhymes near it: see the lesson Hợp Thư.
