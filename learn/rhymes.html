Rhymes

Marking strokes 1 to 6 make a shape a rhyme.

A marking stroke turns a base shape into a rhyme. The shapes come in 11 pairs, the second the mirror image of the first: every tab is one pair, its rows are the strokes 1 to 6 and the plain shape on both (the last tab is the first pair once more, named after its second shape). Stroke 1 always gives the rhyme ông; the plain shape is the vowel alone, stroke 2 adds u or o, the strokes 3 to 6 give the other rhymes. Every row is read with its own shape as the initial, so the first shape reads lông, lanh, lơn, lơi, lơm, lơ. More about ông and the rhymes near it: see the lesson Hợp Thư.
