Tones

Eight tone marks after the syllable.

The tone mark comes after the syllable. A syllable without a mark is ngang; mark 8 writes ngang out. Marks 6 and 7 are the entering tones, sắc and nặng of a syllable that ends in p, t, c or ch: QATT writes its nasal twin (n for t, ng for c, m for p, nh for ch) and the mark makes it a stop, so man becomes mát, mạt.
