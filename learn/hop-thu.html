ông, ong, ung and uông in their short forms.

Four frequent rhymes have no marked shape of their own: they are written with the initial alone. The shape with stroke 1 is the initial + ông (không). The shape with stroke 2, 3 or 4 joined to the same plain shape is the initial + ong, ung, uông (trong, chung, chuông).
