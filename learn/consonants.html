Consonants

Each base shape alone is an initial consonant.

The 22 base shapes stand for the initial consonants of Vietnamese. Where Quốc Ngữ has several spellings for one sound (c, k, q or g, gh), QATT has one shape; the other spellings are shown small under the reading. The shape without strokes, ʔ, is the syllable without a consonant, as in ăn or em.
