國音新字序

Preface to the new national script

厥昔中華*。結繩而治。

* 華 is written without its final stroke here and below.

In ancient times China governed with knotted cords.

後世聖人易之以書契。

The sages of later ages replaced them with written signs.

其蝌蚪縱橫必歸於六書之法。

Their tadpole script, running in all directions, always came back to the method of the six kinds of characters (六書).

迨乎秦政焚書改畫。其反切平上之格。雖畧有存。而聖書之奧掃地盡矣。

When Qin Zheng burned the books and changed the strokes, the rules of fanqie spelling and of the level and oblique tones survived only in part, and the depth of the writing of the sages was swept away entirely.

我國附於中華*。日前只有國音。未有國字。

Our country was attached to China. Formerly it had only the national speech and no national script.

因讀華*書。遂借其字。或諧聲。或繹義。增多其畫以爲南字。

Reading Chinese books, we then borrowed their characters, some for the sound, some for the meaning, and added strokes to them to make Southern characters.

其鄙陋不通。更甚於秦政之書焉。

They are crude and inconsistent, even more so than the script of Qin Zheng.

嘗考之三易卦。始於兩次於四成於八。重之爲六十四。変之爲四千九十六。

I once examined the trigrams of the three Books of Changes: they begin with two, then four, and are complete with eight; doubled they make sixty-four, and changed they make 4,096.

其策則乾坤當朞之日。二篇當萬物之…

…数。

Their stalk counts: those of Qian and Kun match the days of a year, those of the two books the number of the ten thousand things.

樂雖已散亡。而五聲八音十二律。其清濁損益之制。無*非合造化之自然者。

* Source 2 writes 者 instead of 無: 其清濁損益之制者。

Although the Classic of Music is scattered and lost, the five notes, the eight sounds and the twelve pitches, with their rules of clear and turbid, of decrease and increase, are all in accord with the nature of creation.

窃想書契之造。必有一箇簡易之格。而人物聲音靡有遺者。只以秦而泯耳。

I privately think that the making of writing must have had a simple and easy pattern that left out none of the sounds of people and things; it was lost only through Qin.

茲模索音畫創爲一樣書。以便私記已也。

Now, feeling my way through sounds and strokes, I have created a kind of script, only for my own private notes.

豈敢云通倮蠱之聲音乎哉。

How would I dare to say that it renders the voices of all living creatures?

五星聚斗南城居士阮子書。

Written by Master Nguyễn, recluse of Nam Thành, [in the year] the five planets gathered in the Dipper.

本字八音識別

Distinguishing the eight tones of a base character

法於本字上依陰陽四象。識別。每一字各成八字。

Method: on the base character [the tones] are distinguished according to yin and yang and the four images; so every character becomes eight characters.

如同「一東」合一字。惟「同」音其聲在上爲陽。「東」音其聲在下爲陰。

For example, 同 and 東 of the first rhyme, 東, are one character; only, the sound of 同 lies high and is yang, the sound of 東 lies low and is yin.

以陰陽分配則「東董凍篤」四音是陰之「平上去入」。「同洞動獨」四音是陽之「平上去入」。

Divided by yin and yang, the four sounds 東 董 凍 篤 are the level, rising, departing and entering tones of yin, and the four sounds 同 洞 動 獨 the level, rising, departing and entering tones of yang.

以四象識別。則陽用全圈。陰用半圈。

Distinguished by the four images, yang uses a full circle and yin a half circle.

其「同」字全識於其字之左足。「洞」字全識於其字之左肩。

The character 同 is marked with a full circle at the left foot of the character, 洞 with a full circle at its left shoulder,

「動」字全識於其字之右肩。「獨」字全識於其字之右足。

動 with a full circle at its right shoulder, 獨 with a full circle at its right foot;

「東」字半識於其字之左足。「董」字半識於其字之左肩。

東 with a half circle at its left foot, 董 with a half circle at its left shoulder,

「凍」字半識於其字之右肩。「篤」字半識於其字之右足。如下。他倣此。

凍 with a half circle at its right shoulder, 篤 with a half circle at its right foot, as below. The others follow this.

The Cán of đông with the eight tone marks: 東 董 凍 篤 (half circles), 同 洞 動 獨 (full circles).

反切諧音法

The method of fanqie spelling and harmonised sounds

反法。如其字用󰀀󰂜翁󰀐花󰂓󰂚󰁧上字從一東韻內聲上粘一先韻內聲押八下字傍音取之

The fǎn method: for a character one uses 翁 花 反; the upper character is taken from the sounds of the rhyme 一東 and placed first, the lower character from the sounds of the rhyme 一先, rhyming through the eight [tones], by its side sound.

參書下從合体上從枝体畧

Tham Thư, the combined form below and the Chi form above: in brief

書因音成。

Writing comes into being through sound.

中國之音皆出於正。故二十二幹字爲標。足以書之。

The sounds of China all come from the regular [places]; therefore the twenty-two Cán characters serve as marks, and they are enough to write them.

至於夷裔。其音多出於偏旁。湏*用左枝右幹等字爲標。方能書之。

* 湏 or 須?

As for the peoples of the borders, their sounds mostly come from the side; one must use characters with the Chi on the left and the Cán on the right as marks, and only then can they be written.

如󰀅󰂚󰀀󰂛󰁮󰂜⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一音乃嗡圈反合⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一字也

For example, the sound of ⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一 is 嗡 圈 反: combined into the character ⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一.

其音疾語辰舌卷在頷下兩牙⿰广朮闊開上下唇縮八方成音也

Its sound is spoken quickly, the tongue curled under the jaw, the two rows of teeth wide open, the upper and lower lips drawn in on all sides: so the sound is formed.

如󰂊󰂚󰂅󰂛󰀐󰂜音乃𱓄花反法合吧字也

For example, the sound of this character is 𱓄 花 反: by the method combined into the character 吧.

其音疾語辰上下唇緊合聲氣…

…出兩唇微開方成音也。故其音為偏音。其字須參書也。

Its sound is spoken quickly, the upper and lower lips pressed together; the breath comes out with the two lips slightly open: so the sound is formed. Therefore its sound is a side sound, and its character must be written as Tham Thư.

音数

The number of sounds

單幹書協契象。即𧥾別也成一百七十六字。

The single Cán, joined with the marks of the four images (that is, the distinguishing [of the tones]), make 176 characters.

單書協契象。成八百八十字。內有喊字等四十六字其八聲稀微疊語。

The single characters, joined with the marks, make 880 characters (among them 46, such as 喊, whose eight tones are rare, [found] in reduplicated words).

合書左從枝体。右從幹体。協契象。成八百八十字。

The Hợp Thư with the Chi form on the left and the Cán form on the right, joined with the marks, make 880 characters.

合書左從幹体右從枝体。協契象。成一萬八千四百八十字。内有一千九百三十二字其八声稀微疊語。

The Hợp Thư with the Cán form on the left and the Chi form on the right, joined with the marks, make 18,480 characters (among them 1,932 whose eight tones are rare, [found] in reduplicated words).

參書。倣此。

The Tham Thư follow this.
