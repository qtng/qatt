Notes

The evidence concerns the marked Cán only: the heading names the listing by 音, and the characters shown are the marked forms, with the marking stroke at the start position (E18, E37). The unmarked Cán is not shown in the listing; see E34. The same section is the evidence for the names and the sounds (E18).
