Transcription

枝字一百一十字 · 109 Chi, each annotated with a Nôm character

Translation

Heading: 'the 110 Chi characters' (literally 'branch characters, one hundred and ten characters')

Notes

Findings

  • The author gives the number 110 in the heading.

  • The table lists 109 Chi: 77 on p. 3 (7 columns of 11), 32 on p. 4 (11, 11, 10). The systematic arrangement in blocks of five per Cán shows that one was left out by oversight.

  • Source 2 also lists 109. Its third entry from the end of the list is struck out with three dots and written again (C03).

  • The missing form is KHONG MARKED-4 (by the proposer's count, from MARKED-1 as the marked Cán). It lies at the turn from p. 3 to p. 4: KHONG is the 16th Cán, so forms 76 to 80 of a complete table belong to it; p. 3 holds 77 forms, ending with KHONG MARKED-2 and -3, and the 78th, KHONG MARKED-4, is the one not written. Confirmed by the proposer on the images. A slip of the author at the page turn, not a feature of the system.

  • The Nôm annotations next to the forms are not transcribed; deciphering them is not part of the proposal (4.1).
