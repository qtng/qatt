Transcription

嘗考之三易卦。始於兩次於四成於八。重之爲六十四。変之爲四千九十六。

Translation

I once studied the trigrams of the three Changes: they begin with two, then four, and grow to eight; doubled they make sixty-four, varied they make four thousand and ninety-six. [Draft translation, to be checked]

Notes

Transcription of the proposer (transcription/S1-pp1-5.md); 次 is the proposer's reading where the machine reading had 以[?], and 之 (col. 7) is partly covered by the library stamp. 三易 are the three books of changes of tradition. The numbers are those of the trigrams (8), the hexagrams (64 = 8 × 8) and the changes of the hexagrams (4,096 = 64 × 64). The proposal quotes the passage as an inset at the head of section 4, where the script builds its characters by combination from 22 bases.
