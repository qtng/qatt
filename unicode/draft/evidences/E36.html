Transcription

音数

單幹書協契象（即識別也）成一百七十六字。單書協契象成八百八十字（內有喊字等四十六字其八聲稀微置語）。合書左從枝体右從幹体協契象成八百八十字。合書左從幹体右從枝体協契象成一萬八千四百八十字（內有一千九百三十二字其八声稀微置語）。參書倣此。

Text in parentheses: small two-column notes in the manuscript.

Translation

Number of sounds

The single Cán, combined with the marks of the four images (that is, the distinguishing [of the tones]), make 176 characters. The single characters, combined with the marks, make 880 characters (among them 46 characters such as 喊, whose eight tones … [TBD]). The Hợp Thư with the Chi body on the left and the Cán body on the right, combined with the marks, make 880 characters. The Hợp Thư with the Cán body on the left and the Chi body on the right, combined with the marks, make 18,480 characters (among them 1,932 characters whose eight tones … [TBD]). The Tham Thư follow this pattern. [Draft translation, to be checked]

Notes

Findings

  • Each count is eight times a count in the proposal, one for each tone: 176 = 22 × 8 (Cán), 880 = 110 × 8 (single characters; the number of Chi), 880 = 110 × 8 (Chi left, Cán right: Hợp Thư), 18,480 = 2,310 × 8 (Cán left, Chi right: phản thiết pairs).
  • The source thus gives 110 Chi also here, against the 109 entries of the table (E29).
  • The count of 880 with the Chi on the left covers only the 110 same-base pairs; this confirms the narrow reading of Hợp Thư (E07).
  • The section uses 幹 both for the standalone Cán (單幹書, 176 = 22 × 8) and for the component of a combination (合書…幹体), which is always an unmarked Cán (E34). The source thus does not tell the marked and the unmarked Cán apart by name (proposer's observation).
  • 契象 is glossed 即識別也, the term of the tone section 本字八音識別法 (E14).
  • Reading of 協契象 confirmed by the proposer; Source 2 writes the same characters more clearly.

Open
