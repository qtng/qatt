Notes

Notation as in the evidence README: {CU …} standalone unmarked Cán, {CH …} standalone Chi, {PT …} phản thiết pair; the character after the type is the annotation. Positions are counted from the top of the column.

Findings

  • Positions 7–8: a standalone unmarked Cán (翁) before a standalone Chi (宜), not combined.
  • Positions 9–10: the syllable 反 written first in QATT as a phản thiết pair, then as a Han character.
  • Positions 12–14: two standalone unmarked Cán (翁, 翁) before a phản thiết pair (鳥). As a bare component sequence this is CU CU CU CH; only the joiner shows which unmarked Cán combines.
  • Tone marks: every QATT character and component carries the mark of the neutral tone (ngang), except the last two, {PT 衣}{PT 字}, which carry none (E24–E26).
