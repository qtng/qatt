Transcription

[ONG-MARKED-4 J ONG-UNMARKED C KHONG-MARKED-5] 音乃嗡圈反合⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一

Translation

The sound of [the Tham Thư character] is 嗡 圈 反: combined by the method [into the character ⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一]. [Draft translation, to be checked.]

Notes

Location and identification of the first character given by the proposer: it is the sequence ONG-MARKED-4, joiner, ONG-UNMARKED, combiner, KHONG-MARKED-5 (notation of the neutral names of the proposal: J = joiner, C = combiner). The passage is the formula of a Tham Thư character in the form of the phản thiết formulas of E07 and E39, with 反合 and the character ⿰舌⿴⿰片爿一 at the end of the passage. The first character is a QATT character and the last a Chữ Nôm character; both have a Nôm annotation at the right.

Findings

  • The first character is a Tham Thư: its base is the Hợp Thư of ONG (Chi MARKED-4, then the unmarked Cán), and the Chi MARKED-5 of KHONG is the third component above it.

  • The formula names the lower part of the Tham Thư character first and the upper part after it (proposer's reading). The order of the formula is the reading order of the Tham Thư: lower component first, then the upper Chi. This agrees with the encoding order in section 4.3: Hợp Thư, combiner, Chi.

  • The last character is a Chữ Nôm character, 淵 with the radical 舌 instead of 氵 (identification by the proposer). Its pronunciation is unknown. By the phonetic component, as in 淵, it should resemble uyên, with a stop as the initial, which corresponds to the initial of the lower component of the Tham Thư (proposer's inference, not stated by the source).
