QATT sort demo

Every syllable that the draft encoding writes without Tham Thư and without a tone: each Cán with each Chi (a Cán of the same base is written as the Chi alone, 4.2), the Hợp Thư of the Chi with marking stroke 2, 3 and 4 with the Cán of its base, and the unmarked and the marked Cán alone (the syllable of the initial with the rhyme ông, the same reading for both). The reading is that of the decoding qatt from js/parser.js. Sort by the reading, or in binary order of the code points, which is what a text without collation gets (6.4).
