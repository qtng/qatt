Qatt.org Notes

The central app of Qatt.org: the editor and viewer of Quốc Âm Tân Tự texts. Type Vietnamese, read it in QATT, right on your phone.

Open the app Install
Features

Everything to write and read QATT.

Type with Telex

Type Vietnamese as usual; every word becomes QATT at the space.

Direct input

A bar of the 22 base shapes: tap a shape, tap again for its marked forms, digits 1–8 for the tones.

QATT ⇆ Quốc Ngữ

Convert a whole text to Quốc Ngữ and back, with the decoding of qatt.org or Omniglot.

Chữ Nôm

While you type a word, the key bar offers its Chữ Nôm characters; a tap puts one in.

Fonts and layout

The QATT fonts of the website, size, bold, italic, centred and vertical text, for every note of its own.

Styles

Eleven styles with gradients, glowing, embossed and 3D letters.

From the clipboard

Copied QATT or Hán Nôm text shows up when the app opens and becomes a note with one tap.

Search

Search the QATT text and the readings of its characters.

Offline and private

Works without a network; notes are saved automatically on your device, backed up by export and import as JSON.

Install

Like an app on your home screen.

  1. Open Qatt.org Notes in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Choose the menu ⋮ and “Install app” (or “Add to home screen”).
  3. The app updates itself when a new version is out.
