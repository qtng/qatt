QATTKey

The Vietnamese keyboard for Android: write Chữ Nôm, QATT or PA in any chat. It is sent as an image, so everyone can read it without a font.

Download the APK (Android) Install

Free · about 50 MB · beta version

How it works

Type as usual, send in the script you like.

1

Open the field

In any app, tap the 喃 icon in the suggestion bar. A field of its own opens above the keyboard.

2

Type Telex, pick a character

Type Vietnamese with Telex. Below the field come the candidates in PA, QATT and Chữ Nôm; a tap picks one, the space bar converts the word by itself.

3

Send it as an image

The orange send button puts the text in as an image in the style you chose. The receiver sees exactly what you see, without any font.

Styles

Choose the look of your image.

White, Dark, Gold leaf, Cinnabar, Lotus, Sunset, Ocean and Classic (rice paper). Pick one in the menu next to the send button; the field shows the image that will be sent at once, with the same line breaks.

Every app keeps its own choice.

Features

Or as text

Send Chữ Nôm as Unicode text when you like; in an app that takes no images the keyboard sends text by itself.

Your scripts

Switch PA, QATT and Chữ Nôm on or off, and choose what the space bar converts to.

An everyday keyboard

Outside the field it is a full keyboard: Vietnamese, German, English and many more languages, with word suggestions.

Install

Installed in three steps.

  1. Download the APK and open it; Android asks whether this browser may install apps — allow it.
  2. Open QATTKey, switch the keyboard on (Settings → System → Keyboard) and choose it as the active keyboard.
  3. The first time you open the field you choose the scripts you want.
