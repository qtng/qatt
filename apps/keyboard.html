QATTKey

The Vietnamese keyboard for Android: type with Telex, then send the text or an image in QATT, PA or Chữ Nôm to any app.

Download the APK (Android) Install

Free · about 50 MB · beta version

Features

One keyboard, several scripts.

Telex typing

Type Vietnamese as usual on a QWERTY layout, with suggestions and the comma right next to the space bar.

Composition field

Write in a field of its own above the keyboard, with a real cursor, then tap send to put the text into the app.

QATT, PA and Chữ Nôm

Every word has candidates: PA, Chữ Nôm and QATT. You choose which scripts are on, and the space bar turns the word just typed into one of them.

Send as an image

Chữ Nôm and QATT need no font on the receiving side: send an image (PNG) in one of eight styles, or plain text.

Knows what the app accepts

In an app that takes no images the image styles are greyed out and the text is sent.

Short settings

Built on HeliBoard with the ballast removed: fewer settings, sensible defaults, and your other languages one tap away.

Install

Installed in three steps.

  1. Download the APK and open it; Android asks whether this browser may install apps — allow it.
  2. Open QATTKey, switch the keyboard on (Settings → System → Keyboard) and choose it as the active keyboard.
  3. In any text field tap the 喃 icon in the suggestion bar to open the composition field; the first time you choose the scripts you want.

Download the APK (Android)
