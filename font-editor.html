QATT glyphs with KAGE

The symbols drawn by the KAGE engine (glyph outlines from stroke data, as on GlyphWiki), next to the brush outlines of the brush editor. How the fonts are made: see the font page. Click a cell to see it large; drag the dots and lines to edit its KAGE data. The fonts make the halves of a joined pair and of a stack from the single form; a symbol can have its own left or right form where it needs to look different. Prototype.

Marks
One KAGE line per stroke; the first lines are the strokes, then the marks 1–6. Changes apply while you type.
