QATT reference font

The reference font for the QATT Unicode proposal: all 164 characters of the draft block (monospace, one square per character, code points from U+F0000), drawn from the strokes of qatt.js. This is a prototype; stroke widths and proportions may still change. Click a character to add it to the sample field.

The font includes the combining behaviour: A U+F009B (component joiner) B is shown as one square with A in the left and B in the right half; A U+F009A (Tham Thư combiner) B writes B above A (A may be a joined pair); a tone mark combines with the character before it and sits in one of its corners.
