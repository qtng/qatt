Definitions (db/translate_log.sql)

Gap: a word for which the model gave a character of its own (or "?" = it knew none) because the list of the page had no suitable option. Stored with the word before and after (Quốc Ngữ and the character chosen for each; "none" where the word is the first or last), its position and the length of the sentence in words. Only these words, not the sentence. Dictionary = how many characters the dictionary offered for the word: "nothing" means it has none, a number means these did not fit. Contexts = different combinations of the word before and after. At start / at end = how often the word was the first or last of its sentence.

Language: the language of the free translation that was asked for. Errors = no answer (busy, over quota, ...). Fallback = the model's answer was unusable twice. Own characters = share of the words that are gaps. Recorded since 16 Oct 2026.
