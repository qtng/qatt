Tone offsets

Not built yet: this page only holds the plan.

Goal

Fine-tune the place of the small tone marks per letter (Khai first). A tone mark lies over the cell before it, at one place for all letters; where a letter reaches too close to the mark, a small offset for that letter moves the mark away.

Plan

  1. Data: a JSON file, e.g. fonts/tone-offsets.json, per font and tone corner: { "khai": { "upper-right": { "104": [dx, dy], … }, "upper-left": { "22": [dx, dy], … }, … } }. The key of a right corner is the rhyme letter (the letter right before the tone), of a left corner the onset letter (two glyphs before the tone). Values in font units; missing = 0.
  2. Page: a grid of all 154 letter forms, one cell per syllable (onset 63 + the letter as rhyme for the right corners, the letter as onset + rhyme 61 for the left corners), drawn with the font without its tone marks. The tone mark is drawn on top as an SVG of its outline from the font (read with opentype.js, or exported as JSON by the build), at the global place plus the offset of the JSON, so that the page shows exactly what the font will show.
  3. Adjusting: choose a corner and a tone (circle / half ring); click a cell and move the mark with the arrow keys (1 unit, Shift 10) or by dragging; the cell shows its offset and the distance to the letter (red when they touch).
  4. Export: a button copies the JSON of all offsets (only the non-zero ones) to hand over; the page can also load a JSON to continue.
  5. Font: tools/build_font.py reads the JSON and writes a contextual positioning (GPOS) of the mark after those letters (no new glyphs). The right corners look at the rhyme glyph before the tone, the left corners at the onset two glyphs before. Stacks (Tham Thư) and pairs (.markw) are cases of their own.
  6. Elsewhere: js/qatt.js (SVG drawing) has no such rules; if it should follow, it reads the same JSON.
