Tone offsets

Moves the small tone mark after a pair per letter: a tone at the right follows the rhyme (the right half), a tone at the left the onset (the left half). The page draws the letters with the font and the mark from its outline at the global place plus the offset, as the font will show it once it is built with the offsets. Offsets are per style (all weights of a font share them), in font units, y up. Select a cell, then move the mark with the arrow keys (Shift: 10 units) or by dragging it in the large view. Red: the mark overlaps the letter; orange: closer than 20 units. Hand the export over to be committed as fonts/tone-offsets.json and built (tools/tone_offsets.py); single letters, stacks and the wide font keep the global place.
