IDS renderer

Any IDS sequence (Ideographic Description Sequence) drawn by js/ids-render.js: the size of every part in its role is the median of real characters (GlyphWiki), with the GlyphWiki variant of the part for its role. Left: the drawing of the Bookshelf until now (whole glyphs in fixed halves). A part that the data of cdn.qatt.org does not have (a character newer than the data) is read live from GlyphWiki and measured in the browser; it is then sized by the density table only. Middle: every part read this way, none of our measurements (what the renderer does with GlyphWiki alone).

Auto

How much the size of every part comes from the density table (its own layers against the partner's): 0 % = no table, the measured medians and the old fallback (scaled in proportion to the density); 100 % = the table only. Auto (the default of the renderer): the table only for parts without measurements. Changes "New" and the examples (a sequence that is a character of its own keeps its glyph).
